ABOUT ORBIT

A personal digital space for sharing useful resources, study material and media with the people you choose.

The idea

ORBIT is designed as a personal web hub where information can live in one place: study resources, files, photos, videos, notes, games, social links and requests.

Core Features

🌌 Sci-fi space interface with moving stars and layered visual depth
📚 Resource and study-material area
📝 Local notes system
🎮 Game sector with search and filters
🌐 Social hub for your profiles
📡 Request center for friends and visitors
🔐 Optional Google sign-in path
☁️ Ready to connect to Firebase for shared login, files and data
